⚡ Statistical Forecasting of Energy Demand and Grid Stability in Chile's SEN

An end-to-end R project -- ARIMA/SARIMAX, STL decomposition, state space models (ETS), and GARCH -- on the tidyverts/fable ecosystem

This page shows the project's results. The methodology, the design decisions and the limitations are documented in the repository's README.

Real hourly data and day-ahead machine learning (2018-2025)

70,128 real hours of the SEN from the Coordinador's public hourly-generation file, with Santiago temperature and holidays. Every input is at least 48 hours old; LightGBM is trained on 2018-2023, tuned on 2024 and tested on 2025, which it never saw.
